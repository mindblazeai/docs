# Configure AI Skills Connections in LivingContext

<Callout type="info" icon="💡" id="WZgKrljXgk">
  Configure AI Skills Connections in LivingContext to enable AI-powered documentation features for your workspace.
</Callout>

### Step 1 — Navigate to Home

Click the **Home** link in the workspace rail on the left side. This returns you to the main workspace view from wherever you currently are in LivingContext.

<CaptureImage src="./assets/configure-ai-skills-connections-in-livingcontext/step-01.jpg" alt="Step 1" meta="./assets/configure-ai-skills-connections-in-livingcontext/step-01.capture.json" t="678" />

### Step 2 — Open Settings

Click the **Settings** link in the workspace rail. This takes you to the settings area where you can configure workspace-level options and integrations.

<CaptureImage src="./assets/configure-ai-skills-connections-in-livingcontext/step-02.jpg" alt="Step 2" meta="./assets/configure-ai-skills-connections-in-livingcontext/step-02.capture.json" gif="./assets/configure-ai-skills-connections-in-livingcontext/d0567926-1f13-489f-ab40-bfdc416fb652.gif" media="gif" t="15216" />

### Step 3 — Select AI skills

Click the **AI skills** button in the **Your account** section of the sidebar. This opens the AI skills configuration panel where you can manage AI-related settings.

<CaptureImage src="./assets/configure-ai-skills-connections-in-livingcontext/step-03.jpg" alt="Step 3" meta="./assets/configure-ai-skills-connections-in-livingcontext/step-03.capture.json" t="24081" />

### Step 4 — Access Connections

Click the **Connections** link in the section sidebar under AI skills. This displays the connections interface where you can configure and manage your AI Skills integrations.

<CaptureImage src="./assets/configure-ai-skills-connections-in-livingcontext/step-04.jpg" alt="Step 4" meta="./assets/configure-ai-skills-connections-in-livingcontext/step-04.capture.json" t="29938" />

## Summary

You have navigated to the AI Skills Connections section in LivingContext settings. You can now configure the connections needed to enable AI-powered documentation features for your workspace.
