## Installation

This checklist captures everything the on-call engineer verifies before a production release goes out. It exists because the most common source of incidents is not a bad change itself, but a good change shipped without the usual guardrails — a skipped migration dry-run, a feature flag that was never wired up, or a stakeholder who simply never got the heads-up that something was changing under them.

Work through the sections in order. Each one is owned by a specific role, and nothing should be checked off on someone else's behalf. If a step cannot be completed, stop the release and escalate in the #releases channel rather than quietly working around it — a delayed release is almost always cheaper than an incident.

Releases ship every Thursday at 2pm once staging has been signed off by QA and the product owner. Emergency fixes follow the same checklist but may compress the review window with explicit approval from an engineering lead, and every compressed release is written up afterwards.
